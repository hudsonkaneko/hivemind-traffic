## What the ramp tests establish

Only agent_0's request time changes: 0, 38, 44 or 50 seconds. All other requests
remain fixed, including eight ramp requests; there are 44 total. Native ACC and
smooth ACC use matching network/demand bytes on each of seeds 732101 and 732102.
Development uses 732001. The primary horizon is 90 seconds, followed by a
separately accounted drain of at most 90 seconds. Safety modes stay enabled.

Exposure requires a direct leader/follower relationship between agent_0 and a
ramp vehicle, within 120 m on sb2/sb3. Every delayed case meets this gate; the
unshifted controls do not. In the old unshifted development case, agent_0 had
already traversed the merge region before the first ramp vehicle arrived.
Completing eight ramp trips was therefore insufficient evidence of interaction.

All runs have zero collisions, removals and teleports. At the primary horizon,

resolve the latter research requirement or calibrate a human gap model.

## Why the cut-in is abrupt

The matched lateral-off control retains the same event actor and speed schedule
but omits its lateral command. With lateral motion disabled, peak jerk is 2.0.
With the cut-in enabled, the original 33.3704 m/s³ peak is reproduced on the
development and verification fixtures. All 45 trips finish after drain; 41 have
finished at H. The extra request is the independently identified event actor.

At 2.1 s the cut-in actor becomes agent_1's leader. For the resulting 2.2 s step,
the native follow-speed calculation requests −1.83704 m/s², while the comfort
filter still requests +1.3 m/s² after a previous realized +1.5 m/s². SUMO realizes
−1.83704 m/s². The acceleration jump over 0.1 s gives −33.3704 m/s³ jerk. This
supports a conflict between the smoothing request and the new following demand;
the available instrumentation does not uniquely identify SUMO's internal branch.

A diagnostic controller, `follow_cap_acc`, applies the lower of the filtered
and raw follow-speed requests. It removes the one realization mismatch, but
**does not change the realized motion**: both lateral-on and lateral-off pairs
have identical vehicle IDs/lanes and zero maximum differences in speed,
acceleration, x and y over every recorded frame. Its cut-in repeat also matches
exactly. This cap is a diagnostic option, not an adopted comfort fix. Counting
fewer mismatches would have concealed the unchanged jerk failure.
