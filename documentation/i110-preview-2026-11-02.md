## Remaining mechanism and next candidate

All five failing primary peak samples are realized-command mismatches. In ramp-44 at 53.5 s, the preview requests −2.5 m/s² but realizes −4.3698; peak jerk is 18.6976. In ramp-50, the same ceiling realizes −2.7437 at 59.5 s. In cut-in-off, the new peak occurs at 27.5 s near a human vehicle after the preview has changed traffic evolution; it is not the original scripted cut-in event. Cut-in-on’s remaining maximum occurs much later, at 80.2 s. These records expose where realization differs; they do not uniquely identify an internal SUMO safety branch. No terminal-release feasibility conflicts were recorded.

The next design should distinguish path relevance from mere adjacent-path possibility and account for native ACC realization and recovery. Do not tune v1 after seeing these outcomes, remove the adverse controls, or relax safety to make the jerk number pass. Retain the smooth-ACC baseline and predeclare any v2 comparison with the same complete burden reporting.
