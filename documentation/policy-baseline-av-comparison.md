## What the current policy actually does

The actor consumes 45 local/map/merge/mask fields and six 8-field delivered-message slots. A shared 7→16→16 message encoder performs masked mean pooling, adding peer count; the resulting 62 values feed a 128/128 MLP with four outputs: one acceleration mean and three lane logits. Deterministic inference emits normalized acceleration mapped to [-4.5, 2] m/s² and a masked right/stay/left request at 10 Hz.

It is feed-forward: previous-request, cooldown and message-age fields provide limited context, but there is no learned temporal sequence, neighbor-motion forecast or future-trajectory selection. Sensed noncommunicating cars remain visible through local state. V2V is an additional channel, not a substitute for sensing. Camera/LiDAR captures are not actor inputs. SUMO ACC, speedMode 31, laneChangeMode 512 and our guards determine realized motion; SUMO also executes lane centering and lateral motion. The new population critic is a masked set model used only during training; the restored parent originally used the four-agent critic.

## Measured weaknesses before architectural speculation

In the sparse one-AV/24-car cruise case, parent/v02 requested mean acceleration is **0.1774/0.4313 m/s²**, while realized mean acceleration is **0.1403/0.1393 m/s²**. Speed/limit barely changes, **0.8911/0.8920**, but overrides rise **32.625%→86.75%**. The analytical filter never changed the request in this case. This is repeated over-requesting relative to native response, not useful extra progress. Native internal causes are not logged, so not every mismatch is an emergency. [Reproducible journal diagnosis](evidence/policy-baseline-review/diagnosis.json), generated by [derive.py](evidence/policy-baseline-review/derive.py).

Across all sixteen cases, analytical-filter changes drop from 1,188 in the parent to 494 in v02, but native differences rise from 10,765 to 16,822; categories overlap. The combined metric stays unchanged. Native lane-end emergency warnings also require reconciliation with sampled counters before promotion.

The v02 schedule visited only **41 of 174** valid task/population/channel combinations. One-AV cases received **5,049 decisions**, versus **38,404 for six-AV cases**, out of 99,998. Larger cohorts naturally consume more of an agent-decision budget. This exposure imbalance is measurable; its causal contribution remains a hypothesis.

Code/metric inspection also found that 50× acceleration supervision improved reported training-label error while increasing KL early stopping (7/37→20/37 PPO updates). Those losses come from different visited states, not a controlled held-out fit test. PPO, imitation and critic share global gradient clipping. Separate gradient contributions and critic explained variance were not saved, so the exact optimization failure is not yet established. Increasing imitation weight again is not evidence-based.

## Differences from existing published AV approaches

These are architecture and evaluation comparisons, not numerical rankings: CARLA, nuPlan, WOMD and our SUMO fixture have different dynamics and metrics. Public research does not disclose Waymo's entire production driving policy.

| Published approach | Relevant difference from ours | Proposed adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PDM-Closed / PDM-Hybrid, CoRL 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v229/dauner23a/dauner23a.pdf) | Uses route structure and short-horizon trajectory proposals with explicit scoring. Its study distinguishes forecasting fit from actual closed-loop driving. We emit the next action directly. | Compare a competent longitudinal controller plus learned bounded corrections or maneuver selection against the direct actor. This is a new hybrid design, not a post-processing trick to erase overrides. |
| [PlanT, CoRL 2022](https://www.cvlibs.net/publications/Renz2022CORL.pdf) | Uses compact object-level representations with a transformer for planning. Our local slots and pooled V2V summary compress individual relationships. | Test typed ego-relative neighbor/route tokens and masked attention after control calibration. Structured observations are a reasonable starting point; pixels are not mandatory. |
| [Wayformer, Waymo research](https://waymo.com/research/wayformer/) | A motion forecaster using dynamic-agent history and attention to produce multiple possible trajectories, not an ego controller. We have no temporal encoder or explicit forecast. | Add a short observation history first, then test whether uncertainty-aware neighbor prediction helps cut-ins, braking and merges. |
| [SMART, NeurIPS 2024](https://github.com/rainmaker22/SMART) | A multi-agent motion generator trained through next-token prediction over map/motion representations, not a turnkey ego planner. | Consider more realistic reactive background behavior and trajectory data later. Sim-agent realism does not establish safe ego control. |
| [GPUDrive, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/3107ddd4209e5f93c0371425763041a3-Paper-Conference.pdf) | A data-driven multi-agent simulator and RL study using logged maps/traffic with much broader scenario coverage. Our pilot uses 20 synthetic/provisional training cases and a small finite-cohort budget. | Improve scenario diversity and coverage accounting first; benchmark faster simulation only when sample throughput becomes the bottleneck. Its throughput/success claims do not predict our workstation or transfer performance. |
| [TransFuser, PAMI 2023 implementation](https://github.com/autonomousvision/transfuser) | Fuses camera and LiDAR features for imitation-based driving in CARLA. Our actor receives structured simulator state. | Keep sensor learning as a later stage with latency/noise/confidence and tracking validation. Do not add perception errors while the ideal-state controller still regresses. |

## Prioritized improvement experiments

The following are engineering proposals motivated by our evidence and the cited research, not implemented capabilities or new launch authorization.

1. **Calibrate the action interface and safety accounting.** Plot signed requested→filtered→realized errors by speed, gap, road seam and lane end. On training-only fixtures, run bounded command sweeps with the same ACC/safety settings and timestamps. Reconcile native warning events. Exit: explain ordinary response regimes and detect warning events without weakening thresholds or disabling safety.
2. **Measure conflicting objectives before retuning.** Use a fixed training-only diagnostic observation set, taking retained observations where available or reconstructing a reproducible rollout; completed population checkpoints do not retain all historical observation batches. Compare teacher and reward targets, separate PPO/acceleration/lane/critic gradient norms, clipping factors and critic explained variance on that identical set. Test one change at a time, such as a lower actor LR or separated clipping, only after measuring the imbalance. Validation samples must never train the optimizer.
3. **Test a reliable controller plus a small learned correction.** Start from the teacher's measured longitudinal performance, repair its remaining gates, then compare bounded acceleration residuals or target-speed/maneuver proposals with direct acceleration. Log base, residual, proposed, filtered and realized commands; evaluate the complete deployed stack. Keep fallback usage visible and retain speed/pass/merge/cohort constraints.
4. **Balance exposure by active-agent decisions.** Guarantee minimum quotas per task, AV-count and channel bucket, including one AV/no peers. Vary total traffic independently. Add continuous inflow, clustered AVs, lead braking and cut-ins in later declared curricula. Do not imply that an 8-AV pilot proves arbitrary fleet sizes or live controller handoff.
5. **Add temporal context as a separate ablation.** Try a modest history stack or GRU for ego motion, applied commands, neighbor tracks and message ages. Version its reset/inference contract. Compare memoryless/history models on delayed braking and message outages at matched compute.
6. **Improve relevance before increasing network size.** Compare current mean-pooled messages with masked attention using relative geometry and route conflict, retaining distinct sensed-neighbor and received-message channels. Test absent peers, stale packets, more than six candidates and unseen count combinations. Attention does not repair actuator mismatch by itself.
7. **Select on closed-loop performance without hiding regressions.** Freeze a decision rule covering safety events, worst-case and pooled interventions, task success and traffic efficiency. Compare fixed-budget endpoints and repeated seeds only after a successful pilot. Evaluate no-message/impaired-message variants on otherwise identical scenarios. Reserve a fresh final test because counts 3/5/7 are already used for validation tuning.
8. **Transfer to sensors and physical control last.** Add noisy/missing structured observations, then replace fields with calibrated perception/tracking. Validate a trajectory/physical controller before claiming highway-speed camera/LiDAR driving. Use Omniverse for selected closed-loop validation; pose replay is not policy transfer.

**Recommended next work:** items 1 and 2, followed by a small comparison for item 3 if the diagnostics support it. Keep the restored parent frozen. Do not launch a larger training run or copy external weights before establishing a compatible observation/action/dynamics contract.
